Alp Inangu

I am a Research Assistant at Forschungszentrum Jülich (FZJ), IAS-6: Computational and Systems Neuroscience, where I work with the groups of Prof. Abigail Morrison and Prof. Markus Diesmann. I am also an undergraduate student in Computer Engineering at RWTH Aachen University.

My research interests lie at the intersection of computational neuroscience, machine learning, and high-performance computing. I am particularly interested in how the structure and dynamics of biological neural networks give rise to learning and information processing, and how these principles can inform the design of more adaptable and computationally efficient learning algorithms and computing systems.

My current research involves developing infrastructure for closed-loop neural simulations, investigating the performance and scalability of the NEST simulator on high-performance computing systems, and exploring how biologically grounded cortical circuit models can improve the cross-session generalization of brain–computer interface decoders.
